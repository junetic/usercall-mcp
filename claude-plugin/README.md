# Usercall: User Interviews

AI-moderated user interviews and qualitative research for Claude.

Usercall helps you understand **why** users behave the way they do. Create an interview study from a research question, test the interview guide, optionally prepare targeted research triggers, and retrieve evidence-backed themes and verbatim quotes from completed interviews.

## What you get

- Remote MCP tools at `https://mcp.usercall.co` (OAuth)
- Skills:
  - `run-user-research` — broad interview / qualitative research asks
  - `investigate-product-friction` — churn, abandonment, stuck flows
  - `investigate-user-behavior` — spikes, drops, cohort differences
  - `validate-with-users` — evidence before product / UX / pricing decisions
